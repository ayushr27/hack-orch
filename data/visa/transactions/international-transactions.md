# International Visa Transactions

## Using Your Visa Card Abroad

Visa is accepted in 200+ countries and territories. Look for the Visa logo at checkout.

## Foreign Transaction Fees

Many cards charge a foreign transaction fee of 1%–3% of the purchase amount when you use your card outside your home country. Check your cardholder agreement. Some travel-focused cards waive this fee.

## Dynamic Currency Conversion (DCC)

When paying abroad, merchants may offer to charge you in your home currency instead of local currency ("pay in USD" instead of EUR). This is called DCC and typically involves a poor exchange rate. Always choose to pay in the local currency to get Visa's standard exchange rate.

## Notifying Your Bank Before Travel

While Visa cards generally work internationally without pre-notification, some issuers temporarily block unusual activity. To avoid having your card declined:
- Log in to your online banking or app and add a "Travel Notice".
- Or call the number on the back of your card.

## ATM Withdrawals Abroad

Visa debit cards work at international ATMs displaying the Visa/PLUS logo. Fees may include:
- Your issuer's foreign ATM fee ($3–$5 typical).
- The ATM operator's fee (varies).
- A currency conversion fee.

Withdraw larger amounts less frequently to minimize per-transaction fees.

## Card Declined Abroad

If your card is declined:
1. Ensure you notified your bank before travel.
2. Try a different ATM or terminal.
3. Call the international collect number on the back of your card.
4. Have a backup payment method (second card or local currency cash).
