# Visa Card PIN Management

## Setting or Changing Your PIN

Your PIN (Personal Identification Number) is required for ATM withdrawals and some in-store chip transactions.

### How to Change Your PIN
1. Visit an ATM that displays the Visa logo and is affiliated with your issuing bank.
2. Insert your card and enter your current PIN.
3. Select "PIN Change" or "PIN Services" from the ATM menu.
4. Enter your new PIN (4–6 digits depending on issuer).
5. Confirm the new PIN.

Alternatively, many issuers allow PIN changes through:
- Their mobile app (Card Settings → PIN Change).
- Online banking (Card Services → PIN Change).
- Calling the number on the back of your card.

### I forgot my PIN

You cannot recover a forgotten PIN — you must reset it. Contact your card issuer (number on the back of the card) to request a PIN reset. They will verify your identity before allowing the reset.

### PIN locked (too many wrong attempts)

After 3 incorrect PIN attempts, your card is blocked for ATM/chip use. Call the number on the back of your card to unblock. This is a security measure and cannot be bypassed remotely.

## PIN Safety

- Never share your PIN with anyone, including bank employees.
- Cover the keypad when entering your PIN at ATMs.
- Never write your PIN on the card or store it near the card.
