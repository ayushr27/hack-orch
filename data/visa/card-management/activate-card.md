# Activating Your Visa Card

A new Visa card must be activated before first use.

## How to Activate

### Online
1. Go to your card issuer's website (printed on the sticker on your card).
2. Log in or create an account.
3. Navigate to "Card Services" → "Activate Card".
4. Enter the 16-digit card number, expiry date, and CVV.
5. Confirm your billing address.

### By Phone
Call the activation number printed on the sticker on your new card. You will need your card number, expiry, and the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number (SSN) for identity verification.

### Via Mobile App
If your issuer has a mobile app, open it, go to "Cards" → "Activate", and follow the prompts.

## Troubleshooting Activation

### Card not recognized
- Double-check you're entering the full 16-digit number without spaces.
- Ensure you are activating through your card issuer's website, not a third-party site.

### Activation failed — identity mismatch
The information you entered doesn't match what your issuer has on file. Verify your SSN last 4, billing address, and expiry date. If you still can't activate, call the number on the back of your card.

## Using Your Card Before Activation

A card that has not been activated will be declined at point of sale. Always activate before attempting to use.
