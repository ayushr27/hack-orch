# Visa Travel Benefits

## Travel Insurance

Many Visa cards include travel benefits such as:
- **Trip cancellation/interruption**: Reimbursement if you cancel for a covered reason.
- **Travel delay**: Compensation for delays over 4–12 hours (meals, accommodation).
- **Lost/delayed baggage**: Reimbursement for essentials if your bag is lost or delayed.
- **Emergency medical**: Coverage for unexpected medical costs abroad.

Coverage varies significantly by card. Check your Guide to Benefits (in your welcome kit or online account).

## Airport Lounge Access

Premium Visa cards (Visa Infinite, Visa Signature) may include lounge access through programs like Priority Pass or specific airline lounges. Activate the benefit through your card's app or the lounge program's website.

## Hotel and Car Rental Benefits

- **Hotel discounts**: Some cards offer preferred rates at partner hotels.
- **Car rental insurance**: Many Visa cards provide Collision Damage Waiver (CDW) when you pay with the card. This may allow you to decline the rental company's insurance.

To get CDW: decline the rental company's damage waiver at pickup and pay for the rental entirely with your Visa card.

## Emergency Assistance

Visa provides 24/7 emergency assistance worldwide:
- Emergency card replacement (typically within 24–48 hours in major cities).
- Emergency cash disbursement if your card is lost/stolen abroad.
- Medical and legal referrals.

Call: 1-800-847-2911 (US) or collect +1-303-967-1090 (international).
