# Earning and Redeeming Visa Rewards

## How Rewards Work

Rewards programs are run by your card issuer, not Visa directly. Common reward types:
- **Cash back**: A percentage of every purchase returned as cash.
- **Points**: Earn points per dollar, redeemable for travel, merchandise, or cash.
- **Miles**: Earn miles per dollar, primarily for airline redemptions.

## Typical Earning Rates

Rates vary by card. Common structures:
- 1%–2% cash back on all purchases.
- 3%–5% on bonus categories (dining, groceries, travel).
- 1 point per $1 on general spending; 2–5x on bonus categories.

## Checking Your Rewards Balance

Log in to your card issuer's website or app → "Rewards" section. You can also call the rewards number on the back of your card.

## Redeeming Rewards

Redemption options depend on your card:
- Statement credits.
- Direct deposit to a bank account.
- Gift cards.
- Travel bookings through your issuer's portal.
- Transfer to airline or hotel loyalty programs.

Log in to your issuer's rewards portal or app to redeem. Some redemptions process instantly; travel bookings may take 1–3 business days.

## Rewards Expiration

Some rewards programs expire points if:
- The account is inactive for 12–24 months.
- You close the account.
- The program terms change.

Check your cardholder agreement for expiration rules.

## Missing Rewards

If a qualifying purchase didn't earn rewards:
1. Wait 1–2 billing cycles — rewards sometimes post with a delay.
2. Check the merchant category — some categories are excluded.
3. Contact your issuer's rewards support if the issue persists.
