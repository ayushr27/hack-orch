# Visa Fraud Protection

## Zero Liability Policy

Under Visa's Zero Liability Policy, cardholders are not responsible for unauthorized charges when reported promptly. This applies to in-person, online, and phone purchases.

To be covered:
- Report unauthorized charges as soon as you notice them.
- Your account must be in good standing.
- You must not have shared your card details negligently.

## How Fraud Detection Works

Visa and your card issuer use real-time monitoring to detect unusual patterns:
- Purchases in locations far from your usual area.
- Multiple rapid-fire transactions.
- Transactions at high-risk merchant categories.

If fraud is suspected, your card may be temporarily blocked and you may receive a call, text, or email from your issuer to verify activity.

## Reporting Suspected Fraud

1. Call the number on the back of your card immediately.
2. Your issuer will block the card and investigate.
3. You may receive a new card with a new number within 5–7 business days.
4. A dispute is automatically filed for unauthorized transactions.

## Protecting Yourself

- Monitor your account regularly through online banking or your app.
- Sign up for transaction alerts (SMS or email for every purchase).
- Never share your card number, CVV, or PIN.
- Use virtual card numbers for online shopping if your issuer offers them.
- Shred old statements and expired cards.
- Be cautious of phishing emails claiming to be from Visa or your bank.

## Phishing Warning

Visa and banks never ask for your full card number, PIN, or CVV via email or SMS. If you receive such a request, do not respond — report it to your issuer.
