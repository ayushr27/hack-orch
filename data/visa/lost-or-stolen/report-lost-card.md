# Reporting a Lost or Stolen Visa Card

If your card is lost or stolen, report it immediately to prevent unauthorized use.

## How to Report

### By Phone (Fastest)
Call the 24/7 number on the back of your card (or your last statement). If you don't have the card, call your issuer's main customer service line. In the US, Visa's global emergency number is 1-800-847-2911.

### Via Mobile App
Open your card issuer's app → Cards → [Your card] → "Report Lost or Stolen". This blocks the card immediately.

### Online Banking
Log in → Card Services → "Report Lost or Stolen Card".

## What Happens After Reporting

1. Your current card is immediately blocked — no new transactions can be processed.
2. A replacement card is mailed to your address on file (typically 5–7 business days; expedited shipping available).
3. Your account number may change if fraud is confirmed.
4. Pending authorized transactions (e.g., hotel holds) may still post — contact the merchant if needed.

## Zero Liability Protection

Visa's Zero Liability Policy means you are not responsible for unauthorized transactions reported promptly. Report as soon as you notice the card is missing.

## Temporary Virtual Card

Some issuers provide a temporary virtual card number while you wait for the physical replacement. Check your issuer's app.

## Replacement Card Fees

Most issuers send replacements free of charge. Expedited delivery may cost $15–$30. Check with your issuer.
