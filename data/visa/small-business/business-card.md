# Visa Business Cards

## Benefits of a Business Visa Card

- Separate business and personal expenses automatically.
- Earn rewards on common business purchases (office supplies, advertising, travel).
- Issue employee cards with individual limits.
- Detailed spending reports for accounting and tax purposes.
- Higher credit limits than personal cards.

## Applying for a Business Card

Requirements typically include:
- Business name and legal structure (LLC, sole proprietor, corporation).
- Tax ID (EIN) or Social Security Number for sole proprietors.
- Annual business revenue.
- Personal guarantee from the primary cardholder.

Apply through your bank or card issuer's business banking portal.

## Managing Employee Cards

As the primary cardholder, you can:
- Add authorized employees as cardholders with individual spending limits.
- View all employee transactions in real time.
- Lock or unlock individual employee cards.
- Set merchant category restrictions per card.

## Expense Reporting

Most business card portals offer:
- Transaction categorization.
- CSV/OFX export for accounting software (QuickBooks, Xero).
- Receipt matching and upload.
- Monthly and annual summaries.

## Lost or Stolen Business Card

Report immediately by calling the number on the back of the card or through your online banking portal. Employee cards can be frozen individually without canceling the account.
