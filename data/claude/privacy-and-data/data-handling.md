# Claude Data Handling and Privacy

## What data does Anthropic collect?

When you use Claude, we collect:
- Account information (email, name).
- Conversation content (messages you send and Claude's responses).
- Usage metadata (timestamps, device/browser type, feature usage).

## How is my data used?

Conversation data may be used to improve Claude models unless you opt out. You can opt out in Settings → Privacy → "Use my data for model training".

## Data retention

Conversation history is retained until you delete it or your account. After account deletion, data is removed from production systems within 30 days. Backups may retain data for up to 90 days.

## GDPR and data rights

If you are in the EU, you have the right to:
- Access your data.
- Correct inaccurate data.
- Delete your data ("right to be forgotten").
- Export your data.

To submit a GDPR request, email privacy@anthropic.com with your account email and the request type. Requests are processed within 30 days.

## Data security

Anthropic uses industry-standard encryption (TLS 1.2+ in transit, AES-256 at rest). Access to customer data is restricted and logged.

## Do not share sensitive information

Claude is not intended for processing:
- Medical records or health information (HIPAA).
- Financial account numbers or card numbers.
- Social Security numbers or government IDs.
- Passwords or private keys.

If you accidentally shared sensitive information, delete the conversation immediately and change any affected credentials.
