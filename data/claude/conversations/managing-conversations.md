# Managing Conversations with Claude

## Starting a New Conversation

Click the "New Chat" button (pencil icon) at the top of the sidebar to start a fresh conversation. Each conversation has its own context — Claude does not carry memory between conversations by default.

## Conversation History

Past conversations appear in the left sidebar, sorted by most recent. Claude stores your conversations on our servers so you can access them across devices.

To search your conversation history, use the search bar at the top of the sidebar.

## Editing and Regenerating

- **Edit your message**: Hover over a message you sent and click the edit (pencil) icon. Claude will regenerate its response.
- **Regenerate Claude's response**: Click the circular arrows icon below Claude's last response to get a different answer.
- **Copy a response**: Click the copy icon below any Claude message.

## Sharing Conversations

You can share a conversation link (read-only) by clicking the share icon at the top of the chat. Anyone with the link can view the conversation.

## Deleting Conversations

To delete a conversation:
1. Hover over it in the sidebar.
2. Click the three-dot menu → "Delete".
3. Confirm deletion.

You can also delete all conversations from Settings → Privacy → "Clear conversation history".

## Projects

Pro users can organize conversations into Projects. Each Project can have a shared system prompt and uploaded context files that Claude references across all conversations in the project.

To create a Project: Click "New Project" in the left sidebar.
