# Claude Plans

## Free Plan

- Access to Claude with usage limits.
- Limited messages per day.
- Access to Claude Haiku and Claude Sonnet (standard usage).

## Claude Pro

- Higher usage limits (roughly 5x more messages than free).
- Priority access during peak times.
- Early access to new features.
- Access to Claude's most capable models.
- $20/month USD or $18/month on annual plan.

## Claude for Teams (formerly Claude Team)

- Everything in Pro.
- Centralized billing and admin controls.
- Usage analytics per seat.
- $25/seat/month (minimum 5 seats).

## Upgrading to Pro

1. Go to claude.ai.
2. Click your profile (bottom left) → "Upgrade plan".
3. Choose monthly or annual billing.
4. Enter payment details.
5. Subscription activates immediately.

## Cancelling Your Subscription

Go to Settings → Billing → "Cancel subscription". Your Pro features remain active until the end of the billing period. No refunds for partial months.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to your billing address after each payment. You can also download them from Settings → Billing → Invoice history.

## Payment Issues

If your payment fails, Claude automatically retries over 3 days. If all retries fail, your account downgrades to Free. Update your payment method in Settings → Billing → "Update payment method".

We accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, and Discover. We do not process payments ourselves — Stripe handles all transactions.
