# Usage Limits on Claude

## Why do usage limits exist?

Usage limits help ensure fair access for all users and manage infrastructure costs. The limits reset on a rolling basis.

## Free Plan Limits

Free users can send a limited number of messages per day. The exact number depends on message length, model used, and current platform demand. When you reach your limit, you will see a message saying "You've reached your daily message limit". The limit resets after 24 hours.

## Pro Plan Limits

Pro users have significantly higher limits. You can send approximately 5x more messages than free users before hitting a soft cap. If you reach the soft cap mid-day, Claude may temporarily slow responses or prompt you to wait before continuing.

## What counts toward my limit?

- Each message you send to Claude counts as one message.
- Longer messages do not count more than shorter ones.
- Uploading files does not count separately — only the resulting message counts.

## I hit my limit unexpectedly

This can happen if:
- You are on the Free plan and had heavy usage earlier in the day.
- Multiple browser tabs are open and sending messages simultaneously.
- You are sharing an account (against Terms of Service — each user must have their own account).

## Resetting or increasing limits

Free users: Upgrade to Pro for higher limits.
Pro users: Limits reset automatically. If you consistently need more, consider Claude for Teams.
There is no way to purchase additional messages as a one-time add-on.
