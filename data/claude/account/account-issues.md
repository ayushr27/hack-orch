# Account Access Issues

## I can't log in

Common causes and solutions:

### Wrong password
Click "Forgot password?" on the login page. Enter your email, and we'll send a reset link (expires in 30 minutes).

### Account locked
After multiple failed login attempts, your account is temporarily locked for security. Wait 15 minutes, then try again. If you continue to have trouble, use the password reset flow.

### Email not verified
If you never clicked the verification email, check your spam folder. You can request a new verification email from the login page by clicking "Resend verification email".

### Signed up with Google
If you originally created your account with "Continue with Google", you must log in with Google — not email and password. Use the "Continue with Google" button.

## My account was suspended

Accounts may be suspended for:
- Violating Anthropic's Acceptable Use Policy.
- Suspected unauthorized access.
- Non-payment (Pro subscribers).

If you believe the suspension was in error, contact support through claude.ai/support.

## I'm not receiving emails from Claude

1. Check your spam/junk folder.
2. Add no-reply@anthropic.com to your contacts.
3. Check if your email provider is blocking Anthropic's domain.
4. Try a different email address.

## Two-Factor Authentication

To enable 2FA: Settings → Security → "Enable two-factor authentication".

If you lose access to your 2FA device, use a backup code (saved when you set up 2FA). If you don't have backup codes, contact support — account recovery requires identity verification.
