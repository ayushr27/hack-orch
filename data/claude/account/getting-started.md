# Getting Started with Claude

Claude is an AI assistant made by Anthropic. You can access Claude at claude.ai.

## Creating an Account

1. Visit claude.ai.
2. Click "Sign up".
3. Enter your email address.
4. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link.
5. Set a password.
6. (Optional) Connect with Google for single-sign-on.

## Signing In

Go to claude.ai and click "Log in". Enter your email and password, or use "Continue with Google".

## Resetting Your Password

1. Go to claude.ai/login.
2. Click "Forgot password?".
3. Enter your registered email address.
4. Check your inbox for a reset link (expires in 30 minutes).
5. Click the link and set a new password.

If you don't receive the email within a few minutes, check your spam folder.

## Changing Your Email Address

To change your email: go to Settings → Account → Email → "Change email". Enter your new email and confirm with your password. You must verify the new email before the change takes effect.

## Deleting Your Account

Account deletion removes all your conversation history permanently.

To delete: Settings → Account → "Delete account" → confirm.

Under GDPR, you may submit a data deletion request to privacy@anthropic.com.
