# Candidate Account Settings

## Creating an Account

1. Visit hackerrank.com and click "Sign Up".
2. Enter your name, email, and a password (minimum 8 characters).
3. Verify your email by clicking the link sent to your inbox.
4. Complete your profile: add your skills, GitHub link, and resume (optional).

## Changing Your Display Name

1. Log in to HackerRank.
2. Click your profile picture (top right) → "Profile".
3. Click "Edit Profile".
4. Update your "Name" field.
5. Click "Save Changes".

Note: Your username (used in URLs) cannot be changed after account creation.

## Changing Your Email

1. Go to "Settings" from the profile menu.
2. Under "Account", click "Change Email".
3. Enter your new email and current password.
4. Verify the new email via the confirmation link.

## Resetting Your Password

1. Go to hackerrank.com/login.
2. Click "Forgot Password?".
3. Enter your email address.
4. Check your inbox for a reset link (valid for 24 hours).
5. Click the link and choose a new password.

If you don't receive the reset email, check your spam folder. If still not received, try a different email if you may have registered with one.

## Deleting Your Account

Account deletion is permanent and removes all your submission history, certificates, and profile data.

To delete: Settings → Account → "Delete Account" → confirm with password.

Note: Deletion requests under GDPR must include proof of identity. Email privacy@hackerrank.com.
