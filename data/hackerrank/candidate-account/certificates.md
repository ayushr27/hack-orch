# HackerRank Skill Certificates

## What are HackerRank Certificates?

HackerRank Certificates validate your proficiency in specific skills:
- Python (Basic, Intermediate)
- JavaScript (Intermediate)
- SQL (Basic, Intermediate, Advanced)
- Java (Basic)
- Problem Solving (Basic, Intermediate)
- REST API (Intermediate)
- Angular, React, Node.js

## How to Earn a Certificate

1. Go to hackerrank.com/skills-verification.
2. Select the skill and level.
3. Take the timed assessment (usually 90 minutes).
4. Pass the minimum score threshold (typically 70%).
5. Download your certificate immediately or from your profile later.

## Certificate Validity

Certificates do not expire. However, employers may value recently earned certificates more.

## Retaking a Certificate Test

You must wait 6 months between attempts for the same skill/level. There is no way to bypass this cooldown.

## Sharing Your Certificate

Each certificate has a unique shareable URL. Share it on LinkedIn or add it to your resume. Employers can verify authenticity via the link.

## Certificate Not Appearing

After passing, the certificate usually appears within 15 minutes. If it doesn't:
1. Refresh your profile page.
2. Check if you met the minimum score threshold.
3. Clear browser cache and try again.
4. If still missing after 2 hours, contact support@hackerrank.com with your submission ID.
