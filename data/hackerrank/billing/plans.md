# HackerRank for Work: Plans and Billing

## Available Plans

### Starter
- Up to 50 candidates/month.
- Access to basic question library.
- Email support.

### Professional
- Up to 250 candidates/month.
- Full question library including advanced algorithms and project tests.
- Priority email support.
- ATS integrations (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday).

### Enterprise
- Unlimited candidates.
- Custom question creation with SLA guarantees.
- Dedicated customer success manager.
- SSO and advanced security controls.
- Custom contracts and invoicing.

## Billing Cycle

Plans renew monthly or annually. Annual plans receive a 20% discount.

## Upgrading or Downgrading

To change your plan:
1. Go to Settings → Billing.
2. Click "Change Plan".
3. Select the new plan.
4. Confirm the prorated charge or credit.

Downgrades take effect at the end of the current billing period.

## Invoices

Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and available in Settings → Billing → Invoice History.

## Cancellation

To cancel, go to Settings → Billing → Cancel Plan. Your account remains active until the end of the paid period. No refunds for partial months.

## Payment Methods

We accept major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) and ACH bank transfers (Enterprise only). We do not store card numbers — payment is processed via Stripe.
