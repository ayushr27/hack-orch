# Company Admin: Creating Assessments

## Getting Started

Company admins access the dashboard at hackerrank.com/work. You must have an active recruiter plan.

## Creating a New Assessment

1. Click "Assessments" in the left sidebar.
2. Click "Create Assessment".
3. Choose a template or start from scratch.
4. Add questions from the library or create custom questions.
5. Set time limit, attempt limit, and deadline.
6. Configure candidate settings (webcam, copy-paste, IDE preferences).
7. Preview the test as a candidate.
8. Click "Publish".

## Inviting Candidates

1. Open the assessment and click "Invite Candidates".
2. Enter email addresses (one per line or CSV upload).
3. Set the invitation expiry date.
4. Click "Send Invites".

Candidates receive a link valid until the deadline. You can resend invites from the candidate list.

## Viewing Results

Results appear in the "Candidates" tab of each assessment. You can:
- Filter by score, status (invited, started, completed, cheating flagged).
- Export results to CSV.
- View detailed playback of a candidate's typing and actions.

## Plagiarism Reports

The system flags candidates with high similarity scores. To view:
1. Open the candidate's result.
2. Click "Plagiarism Report".
3. Review the matched submissions.

Note: High similarity does not automatically mean cheating. Common algorithms submitted by many candidates will show false positives.
